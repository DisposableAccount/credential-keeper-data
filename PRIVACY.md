# Credential Keeper Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026

Credential Keeper does not collect, store or share any personal information with the developer.

## Your data stays with you
- Your licenses, CME records, expenses, education records and documents are stored on your devices and in your own private iCloud database (Apple CloudKit). The developer cannot see or access them.
- Settings sync through your own iCloud account.
- If you add due dates to a calendar, they are written to a calendar you choose, through your own calendar account.
- If you email a credentialing packet, the app opens your own mail app with the PDF attached. Nothing passes through the developer.
- If you turn on Copy Documents to iCloud Drive, a copy of your documents is written to the Credential Keeper folder in your own iCloud Drive. The developer cannot see or access it.
- When you import a file or photo, the app can remove the original after the document is saved (Settings › Sync & Backup › Delete Originals After Import). Files go to the Trash; photos go to Recently Deleted after the system asks you to confirm. Turn this off to keep originals.

## What the app downloads
- Every few months the app downloads a public list of board renewal requirements from GitHub. No personal information or identifiers are sent with that request.

## Diagnostics
- Crash and performance reports from Apple (MetricKit) stay on your device. A diagnostics report leaves your device only if you choose to share it from Settings › Diagnostics.
- If you have opted in to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may provide anonymous crash and usage statistics to the developer.

## No tracking
- No accounts, no advertising, no third-party analytics and no tracking.

## Permissions
- Camera: to scan documents. Face ID / Touch ID: to lock the app. Calendars: to add due dates, only if you turn it on. Notifications: for expiration reminders.
- Photo Library: only to move a photo you imported to Recently Deleted after it's saved in the app. The app doesn't read or upload any other photos.

## Deleting your data
Delete records in the app, or use Settings › Reset App…, which erases everything on every device signed in to your iCloud. You can also delete the app's data in your device's iCloud settings.
